A taste of the island

Restaurant & Bar

Good food, refreshing drinks and beautiful beachfront views over Saracen Bay.

Asian & International Cuisine

Enjoy breakfast, sandwiches, pizzas, burgers, pasta, salads and Western favourites, along with traditional Khmer and Asian dishes.

Our bar also serves a wide range of alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks.

How to Order

Visit the bar and restaurant to place your order.

Food can then be delivered to your room or another location on the island.

Order before 7pm for free delivery. After 7pm, a $1 delivery charge applies.
